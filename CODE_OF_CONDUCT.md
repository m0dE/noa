# Code of conduct

Noa follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and no harassment of any kind.

Report unacceptable behavior to support@noa.bot. Reports are handled in confidence, and the
maintainers may remove comments, issues or pull requests, or block accounts, that break it.
